Who We Are

Two Creators, One Vision

BroTales Studio was born from a shared passion of two friends to pursue quality visual storytelling. What began as a serious hobby and interest became a full-service photography, videography and AI media studio. Delivering Reliability x Creativity in all these areas has been the core tenet of the company since its founding.

01

Anthony L Tjandra

Online Editor & Post-Production

Anthony's interest in videography began the day he received his first camera phone — a spark that never faded. What started as a hobby grew into a deep passion that took him across borders, working both locally and in overseas environments from Singapore to China.

Today he serves as the studio's Online Editor, overseeing and handling most of the Post-Production work that shapes every final deliverable.
